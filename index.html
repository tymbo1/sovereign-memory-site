RETRIEVAL, NOT GUESSWORK

Find the moment, not merely the conversation.

Hybrid retrieval combines exact phrases with semantic similarity. Results can point to the matching passage, message, timestamp and original source instead of presenting an entire unrelated thread.

Private by construction

The vault lives on your machine. Local inference remains possible, and external destinations are optional rather than assumed.

⟡

Evidence with a lineage

Original messages stay distinguishable from summaries, derived memories and model-generated conclusions.

A visible boundary

Disclosure is a decision, not a side effect.

When a model needs context, Sovereign Memory can show the destination, the selected payload and the character count before dispatch. The vault itself never leaves.
